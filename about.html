Who We Are

RAU Metals is a leading manufacturer of aluminum extrusion profiles, serving global markets with precision-engineered solutions for industrial, construction, solar and automotive sectors.

With over 20 years of experience, our state-of-the-art facility produces custom profiles to exact specifications, backed by rigorous quality control and ISO certification.

We export to over 30 countries across Europe, the Middle East and beyond, building lasting partnerships based on quality and reliability.
