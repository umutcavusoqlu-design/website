QUALITY CONTROL

Our Quality Control Process

1
Incoming Material Inspection

[Placeholder] All incoming billets are tested for chemical composition using a spectrometer. Certificates of conformance are verified against order requirements.

2
In-Process Checks

[Placeholder] Dimensional sampling is performed at the press and after each surface treatment stage. Any out-of-tolerance profiles are immediately quarantined.

3
Mechanical Testing

[Placeholder] Tensile strength, yield strength and hardness are measured per batch to verify compliance with the specified temper (T5, T6, etc.).

4
Surface Quality Inspection

[Placeholder] Visual and instrumental inspection of anodised and powder-coated surfaces to check coating thickness, adhesion and appearance.

5
Dimensional Verification (CMM)

[Placeholder] Critical profiles are measured on a Coordinate Measuring Machine to verify cross-section dimensions against approved drawings.

6
Final Release & Documentation

[Placeholder] Batches are released only after all inspections pass. Material test reports and certificates of conformance are issued with every shipment.
